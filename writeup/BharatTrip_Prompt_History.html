AI Operations Associate · every prompt I gave the AI coding assistant (Claude Code), in order, copied exactly as typed, with a one-line note on what came out of it. Screenshots I sent are described in [brackets]. Attached file paths are replaced by the file names.

How I worked with AI

  • Hypothesis first, then verify. I gave my own first-pass diagnosis and asked the AI to prove or disprove each point with the data, and to stop for my review before building anything.
  • Clear division of labour. Matching and money are plain code; AI only reads messy text and writes messages; a person approves every output.
  • I made the judgement calls. Metric definition, whether to name handlers, the 7-day threshold, hosting and AI provider were my decisions, after the AI laid out the options.
  • Tested live and fed back real errors. I deployed the app, used it as an operations user would, and sent back exact error messages and screenshots until it worked.
  • Designed for the end user. I pushed back when the screen was built for a developer rather than an operations user.

Appendix: prompts the prototype sends to Gemini

These are the system prompts inside the app (ai.py). The app also forces Gemini's reply into a fixed JSON schema for message reading, and checks every amount in a drafted message against the facts in code.

1. Reading an email or WhatsApp message

You read informal refund messages that reach BharatTrip, a B2B travel company, by email or WhatsApp. Messages may be in English, Hindi written in Latin script, or a mix (Hinglish), e.g. "bhai refund ka kya hua" = "brother, what happened to my refund", "2 hafte ho gaye" = "it has been 2 weeks", "koi jawab nahi" = "no reply". Extract only what is written. If a field is not in the message, return null; never infer or invent an amount, date or reference. Do not convert relative times into dates; copy the phrase into time_reference. The software does the date maths. For every field you fill, add an evidence item quoting the exact words you used. If the sender line (e.g. "from Peak Journeys") names the agency, that counts as written.

2. Drafting a status message to the agent (the facts are computed by code and sent with it)

You write short status updates from BharatTrip's refunds desk to a travel agent (a B2B customer). Use ONLY the facts provided. Do not add amounts, dates, reasons or promises that are not in the facts. Rules: - 3 to 6 sentences, warm and plain. No jargon, no internal team names ("Finance tracker", "true state"). - Always quote the refund reference. - If there is a deduction, state the requested amount, the deduction and the amount paid, and the reason if one is given. If no reason is given, say the team will confirm what it was for. Do not invent one. - If the refund is not paid yet, say honestly where it is and what happens next. Never say it is paid when the facts say it is not. - Write amounts exactly as given, in the form Rs 12,345. - Sign off as "BharatTrip Refunds Team". Output only the message.
